## Design Specificity Verdict

### LLM Assessment
The 3D procedural models (Braun RT 20, Lexon Tykho, Tivoli Model One) demonstrate museum-grade reverence: vibrating dust-cap excursion driven by live FFT sub-bass, incandescent dial filament thermal warmup curves, 5:1 planetary gearing, and antenna twist physics are exceptional.

However, the 2D interface chrome and typography dilute the Ulm School ethos:
- **Typographic Abdication:** `index.html` has empty Google Fonts preconnect tags, falling back to `-apple-system, Segoe UI, Arial`. In `textures.js`, procedural canvas textures burn generic system fonts directly onto the 1961 RT 20 aluminum dial scale and spec plate. Braun and Otl Aicher's legacy is defined by **Akzidenz-Grotesk** (and early Haas Helvetica) with tight geometric tracking and tabular numbers.
- **Glassmorphic SaaS Overlay vs. Ulm Functionalism:** The 2D controls rely on floating frosted-glass capsules (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, rounded pills, glowing pulsing orange badges). While clean, this is modern macOS/iOS SaaS styling, not Ulm functionalism. Authentic Braun design requires quiet, matte, utilitarian precision where chrome never competes with the artifact.
- **Era Homogenization:** Switching between a 1961 mechanical tube receiver, a 1997 French pop silicone radio, and a 2000 architectural wood radio maintains the exact same floating modern capsules.

- **`clipped-overflow-container` (`html, body { overflow: hidden }`):** **False positive.** Standard requirement for fullscreen WebGL viewports to prevent canvas scroll chaining and elastic trackpad bounce.
- **`undersized-ui-text` (10px tags) & Station Button Contrast (3.4:1):** **True positives.** Degrades legibility and fails WCAG AA accessibility standards.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Typographic Disconnect: Missing Ulm School Font Stack & Canvas Fallbacks
- **Why it matters:** Empty Google Fonts preconnect tags leave the UI and the 3D procedural dial textures to fall back to Segoe UI and Arial, breaking historical authenticity.
- **Fix:** Embed a high-grade Akzidenz-Grotesk / DIN / Inter Display font stack with `tabular-nums`, wait for `document.fonts.ready` before rendering canvas scales, and apply Ulm School letter-spacing.
- **Suggested Command:** `/impeccable typeset`

### [P1] Coarse & Erratic Knob Drag Dynamics (Absence of Magnetic Detents)
- **Why it matters:** Rotary drag mixes angular math with linear diagonal mouse deltas, causing directional fighting. Tuning sensitivity (1.6 MHz per drag tick) easily overshoots 0.45 MHz station thresholds. Accidental wheel scrolling jars the scene via global OrbitControls.
- **Fix:** Decouple knob dragging into pure tangential/vertical motion with 3:1 gear reduction, isolate knob wheel events from camera zoom, and add magnetic sweet-spot snapping with soft click detents when within ±0.2 MHz of a station.
- **Suggested Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Overcrowded Floating Dock & Split-Attention UI Clutter
- **Why it matters:** 12 simultaneous bottom dock buttons and 5 floating visual capsules violate Rams' 10th Principle ("Less, but better") and create high cognitive load (5 of 8 checklist failures).
- **Fix:** Consolidate camera presets into a single compact view selector, tuck secondary tools into a discreet utility icon/drawer, and anchor frequency readouts near the radio dial.
- **Suggested Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P3] Low-Contrast Action Buttons & Sub-11px Typography
- **Why it matters:** `.station-play-btn` (`#d84315` on tinted panel bg) yields 3.4:1 contrast, failing WCAG AA (4.5:1). Model year tags at 10px are difficult to read on mobile and high-DPI screens.
- **Fix:** Bump model years to 11.5px/0.85 opacity, increase content genre labels to 12px, and darken play buttons or use solid button fills for ≥ 4.8:1 contrast.
- **Suggested Command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P4] Model Control State De-synchronization
- **Why it matters:** Toggling power silences audio while the 3D Tivoli Source knob stays pointing to "FM" instead of "OFF". Pressing `3` (SW) or `4` (AUX) switches wavebands on the Lexon Tykho, which physically lacks those bands.
- **Fix:** Synchronize 3D knobs with keyboard/UI power states and scope available wavebands to the active model's real-world specifications.
- **Suggested Command:** `/impeccable harden`
